# Current Phase-1 nonclaims

PCFW verifies claim conditions relative to a formally specified learned
observation regime; it does not thereby verify the learned system in its entirety.
Phase 1 remains **OPEN**. This current implementation companion supplements the
normative [NON_CLAIMS.md](NON_CLAIMS.md); it does not broaden the claim scope.

Current evidence does not establish:

- verification of the learned model as a whole;
- correctness of the learning procedure or training data;
- correctness of arbitrary external observations;
- claims beyond the formally specified learned observation regime;
- transcript faithfulness from repeatability, parsing, hashing or replay;
- discharge of theorem premises from `Closed under the global context`;
- cryptographic collision resistance from mere digest determinism;
- general executable correspondence from extraction or agreement on test cases;
- correspondence for the retained historical native-integer demo;
- Rocq-domain validity of arbitrary raw OCaml bigint inputs;
- capture/replay correspondence or complete parser/loader conformance;
- byte-identical reproducible binaries, production-system security or certification;
- Phase-2 properties, or completion of Phase 1 from a passing release gate.

Version 0's `EXACT` branch remains unreachable. Finding no witness does not prove
fibre constancy. Mathematical and executable conclusions retain their stated
policy, domain, binding and F.3 premises. See [TRUST.md](TRUST.md).

All required extracted paths now use the existing arbitrary-precision mappings.
The structural gate, finite differential cases and empty-campaign integration
harness strengthen executable evidence within their stated boundaries; they do
not discharge F.3 or establish general executable equivalence.
